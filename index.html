About our shop

Who we are and what we repair.

Sagar Electricals is a motor repair shop in Kushtagi. We rewind electric motors and change worn or damaged motor parts, so your motor works properly again.

We repair small 1 HP motors, 5 HP and 10 HP motors, and large mixer grinder motors. We work on all types of motors except borewell motors.

Bring your motor to the shop. We will check it and explain what work it needs.
